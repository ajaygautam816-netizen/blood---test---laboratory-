PATIENT INFORMATION

A little preparation goes a long way

Preparation depends on the test. Always follow the specific instructions given by your doctor or the laboratory.

🍽️
Fasting instructions

Some tests may require fasting; many do not. Confirm the required duration and whether water is allowed.

💊
Medicines & supplements

Tell the healthcare professional about medicines and supplements. Do not stop prescribed medicines unless advised.

🧴
Urine sample collection

Ask for the correct container and clean-catch instructions. Follow the timing and transport directions provided.

📞
Before you visit

Call or WhatsApp to confirm test availability, price, sample requirements and reporting expectations.
