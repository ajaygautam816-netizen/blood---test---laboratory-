Ask about my test ↗

Accreditation applies to a specific laboratory and scope.

MAXO DIAGNOSTIC LAB is presented here as a sample collection and enquiry point. Do not assume that a certificate held by a partner testing laboratory automatically accredits MAXO as a separate testing laboratory.

If accreditation is relevant to your test, ask us to identify the laboratory processing it and confirm the current certificate, location and approved test scope. A certificate’s validity and scope should be checked against the issuing authority’s current records.

Transparency note: This website does not claim that MAXO DIAGNOSTIC LAB itself holds a separate NABL medical-laboratory accreditation. Please verify the applicable laboratory and scope before booking.
Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. They are a guide based on the supplied price list. Confirm the latest price and availability directly before booking.

Can I book on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use any WhatsApp button to send an enquiry. The team can confirm test availability and next steps.

Is home sample collection available?

Please contact the team to confirm whether home collection is available for your address, test and preferred time.

Do I need to fast before a test?

Some tests may require preparation and others may not. Ask the lab or your healthcare professional for test-specific instructions before giving a sample.

Can you confirm package inclusions?

Yes—contact us to confirm the current test-wise inclusions, availability and package price before booking.

Can the lab interpret my report?

Test results should be interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional in the context of your symptoms and medical history.
