Quick checklist

A few simple things to check

These general pointers help you ask the right questions—follow the instructions given for your specific test.

  • Fasting: do not fast unless the specific test or your clinician requires it; confirm the duration in advance.
  • Medicines: do not stop prescribed medicines unless your clinician tells you to.
  • Water: ask whether plain water is permitted during any required fasting period.
  • Urine sample: ask for the correct container and whether a clean-catch or midstream sample is required.
  • Previous reports: keep relevant old reports available for your doctor to compare.
  • Timing: confirm sample collection hours, price, test components and expected report time directly with MAXO.

Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a prescription for every test?

Requirements depend on the test and local rules. For advice on which investigation is appropriate, speak with a qualified clinician.

Should I fast before a blood test?

Not every blood test requires fasting. Confirm the specific test instructions with the lab or the clinician who ordered it.

Can I choose a test based only on symptoms?

Symptoms can have many causes. Avoid self-diagnosing or ordering a large panel without guidance; a clinician can help decide which tests are relevant.

Does a high or low result always mean disease?

No. Results need to be interpreted alongside reference ranges, symptoms, medical history, medicines and sometimes repeat or additional testing.
